# 01 — Reorder a Map's Graphs

**Status:** ready-for-human

**What to build:** an operation that lets the author change the order of the Graphs a Map owns.

The accepted design is R25 in `docs/agents/maps-and-graphs.md` (ADR 0040). Graph order is authored: creating a Graph appends it, deleting one keeps the survivors' order, and manual reordering is a separate operation. No such operation exists in code.

**Open decision: the gesture and its surface.** Where and how the author reorders, for example in the Dock's Graph `ChoiceMenu` (`packages/app/src/components/CommandDockMapGraph.tsx`), by drag, by keyboard, or both. It goes through `$shadcn-first-ui`.

**What it needs in the domain:** a Space Authoring operation, completing as one Edit, that moves one Graph to a new position in its Map's `graphs` list. It leaves every other Map, the Active Graph and every Graph's Edges and colour unchanged.

**Lands after:** `.scratch/graph-colour/issues/04-a-graphs-colour-does-not-depend-on-its-position.md`. Until that is fixed, a Graph with no stored `color` takes its colour from its position, so reordering would recolour it.

**Acceptance:**

- Reordering moves the chosen Graph to the chosen position in its Map's `graphs` list and leaves the other Graphs in their relative order.
- The Active Graph, every Graph's Edges and colour, and every other Map are unchanged.
- The new order is what the Dock and the Map overview show. It persists across a reload and round-trips through Export and Import.
- Moving a Graph to the position it already holds completes as `unchanged`.
- The reordering entry is removed from the contract's "Accepted, not built" section in the change that verifies this.
