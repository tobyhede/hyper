## Review notes that do not merit another ticket

- The comment rule itself is useful; adding a new word-ban scanner would reproduce the brittleness this effort is trying to reduce.
- `CoordinatedCommit` retains mutable private collections, but their owner and lifetime are explicit. That is not independently a defect or a reason to demand a different state-machine library.
- `dock-chrome.ts` and `resource-placement.ts` are still substantial modules. Do not split them again merely to move line counts; require evidence of mixed responsibilities or costly changes first.
- The local test result contains warnings, not failures. A slow or loaded run is not sufficient evidence to increase timeouts or retries.
- The 1 MiB commit limit is a visible product constraint, but this review did not establish a new defect around it. Include payload size in ticket 17's measurements rather than prescribing a delta protocol now.
