# Opening in place

Opening a Thing reads it where it sits. The body stays Markdown, and Edit
swaps that body for the source editor without closing the Thing.

This paragraph is here so opening has more than a heading to show: the
Deep dive is the Space you Enter when you want to verify that an Open Thing
still draws, still scrolls, and still offers Close.

A third paragraph keeps the panel tall enough that a small viewport has
something to scroll, the same job D does on Collection 1, without borrowing
that Thing's title.
