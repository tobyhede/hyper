title: Try it
---

1. Pick a route in the toolbar — every route stays drawn, and yours is emphasised
2. Click a card to open it and read its Markdown source
3. Press `Esc` to close it
4. Drag a card to move it; the arrangement is committed to the live backend
5. Press **Present** and walk the route with the arrow keys

That's the whole idea: *portable content, graph structure, curated routes.*
